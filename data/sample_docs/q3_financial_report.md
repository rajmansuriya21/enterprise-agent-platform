# Q3 2024 Financial Report

## Executive Summary

Q3 2024 represented a strong quarter for the company, with total revenue reaching **$165.8M**, a **12% increase** year-over-year. This growth was driven primarily by the Enterprise Suite Pro product line and expanding partnerships in the East and North regions.

## Revenue Breakdown by Region

| Region | Q3 Revenue    | YoY Growth | Market Share |
|--------|--------------|------------|--------------|
| North  | $45.2M       | +15%       | 28%          |
| East   | $52.1M       | +18%       | 32%          |
| West   | $38.6M       | +8%        | 23%          |
| South  | $29.9M       | +5%        | 17%          |

## Product Performance

### Top Performers
1. **Enterprise Suite Pro** — $67.2M revenue (+22% QoQ)
2. **Security Audit Package** — $28.5M revenue (+35% QoQ)
3. **API Gateway Pro** — $24.8M revenue (+12% QoQ)

### Growth Opportunities
- The ML Pipeline Starter product showed **45% growth** in new user sign-ups.
- Cloud Storage subscriptions increased by **28%**, indicating strong product-market fit.

## Key Metrics

- **Gross Margin**: 72.3% (up from 69.8% in Q2)
- **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)**: $1,250 (down 8% QoQ)
- **Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)**: $18,500
- **LTV/CAC Ratio**: 14.8x
- **Net Revenue Retention**: 118%
- **Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)**: $18.4M

## Operational Highlights

- Headcount grew to **150 employees** across 8 departments.
- Engineering team completed migration to **microservices architecture**.
- Customer satisfaction score (CSAT) maintained at **4.6/5.0**.
- System uptime achieved **99.97%** for the quarter.

## Outlook

Q4 projections indicate continued growth trajectory with an estimated revenue of **$178-185M**, supported by:
- New enterprise contracts in the pipeline
- Expansion of the security product line
- Upcoming product launch (Data Integration Toolkit v2.0)
