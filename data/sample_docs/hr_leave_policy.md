# Enterprise Leave Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy outlines the company's leave management framework, applicable to all full-time and part-time employees. It is designed to promote work-life balance while ensuring business continuity.

## 2. Annual Leave (Paid Time Off)

- **Full-time employees** are entitled to **20 working days** of paid annual leave per calendar year.
- **Part-time employees** receive a pro-rata allocation based on their contracted hours.
- Annual leave must be **requested 2 weeks in advance** for periods of 3+ consecutive days.
- A maximum of **5 unused leave days** may be carried over to the next calendar year.
- Unused leave beyond the carryover limit will be forfeited on December 31st.

## 3. Sick Leave

- All employees are entitled to **12 paid sick days** per year.
- A **medical certificate** is required for absences exceeding **3 consecutive days**.
- Sick leave cannot be carried over or converted to cash.
- The company reserves the right to request a fitness-for-duty evaluation before an employee returns from extended sick leave.

## 4. Parental Leave

- **Primary caregiver**: 16 weeks of paid leave at full salary.
- **Secondary caregiver**: 4 weeks of paid leave at full salary.
- Parental leave must be taken within 12 months of the child's birth or adoption date.
- Employees may request a flexible return-to-work schedule for up to 6 months post-leave.

## 5. Bereavement Leave

- **Immediate family** (spouse, children, parents, siblings): 5 paid days.
- **Extended family** (grandparents, in-laws, aunts, uncles): 3 paid days.
- Additional unpaid leave may be granted at the manager's discretion.

## 6. Public Holidays

The company observes **10 public holidays** per year as defined by the local jurisdiction. When a public holiday falls on a weekend, the following Monday will be observed as the holiday.

## 7. Leave Request Process

1. Submit a leave request through the HR portal at least **2 weeks in advance** (for planned leave).
2. Obtain approval from your **direct manager**.
3. For urgent leave (e.g., sick leave), notify your manager by **phone or email** before your scheduled start time.
4. HR will confirm the approved leave within **2 business days**.
